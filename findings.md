# Student Performance Analytics - Findings

## Finding 1: Average Scores

Reading had the highest average score among the three subjects, while math had the lowest average score.

## Finding 2: Data Quality

The dataset contained no missing values and no duplicate records.

## Finding 3: Gender Analysis

Female students scored higher on average in reading and writing, while male students scored higher in mathematics.

## Finding 4: Test Preparation

Students who completed the test preparation course generally achieved higher scores.

## Finding 5: Correlation

Reading and writing scores showed the strongest positive correlation

## Conclusion

This project analyzed student performance using Python and pandas. The analysis showed that the dataset was clean, reading and writing scores were strongly related, and students who completed the test preparation course generally performed better.
